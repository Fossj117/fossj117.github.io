One of the reasons that I post so much publicly on this site is that I want to live in a world where people can feel comfortable sharing imperfect, incomplete, and developing thoughts, questions, ideas, reflections. I want it to be "okay" to be wrong, even publicly. We are all learning here, trying to figure things out, make sense of things. 

Of course, there can and should be disagreement, and even accountability when appropriate. But I'd like to see a world that engages with these things by seeking to understand and helping others understand. If someone does or says something wrong, let's help them be better. Justice, yes -- but also peace and repair.
