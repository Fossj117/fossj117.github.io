Notes on [*Alien*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alien_(film)):  
* **Director**: Ridley Scott
* **Cinematographer**: Franz Lustig
* **Watch date**: 7/20/26 at Brattle
* **Rating**: 8.7/10  

**Notes**:

A classic that I had never seen. I enjoyed it for sure, I appreciated the pacing and the development of the story, much like a horror movie with slow escalation and moments of uncertainty, taking foot on and off the gas; lots of good foreshadowing. Uncertainty that builds slowly into clarity. Feels archetypal as a horror movie form. Also manages to handle the "why would you do that?" horror movie issue with the science officer twist. 

Sigourney Weaver is good in it. 

For me also always fun to see past imaginations of future technology. Notable here was the analog physicality and size of things in the presentation. The dedicated room with lots of flashing lighted buttons that is the home of the super computer. The physical puzzle with dedicated buttons, shafts and levers that has to be solved to initiate the self-distruct sequence.
