I am interested in doing an urban photo series about the boundaries between the physical human infrastructure of the city, and the "natural" world that exist in and around it, especially as represented by plants. Plants and tress and vienes that grow up, in, around and through grates, fences, signs, curbs. A lot of city infrastructure is itself about boundary making. Cars can go here but not there. Stop here, go there. Plants are often expected to inhabit particular spaces, within particular boundaries, and yet also are not beholden to them in any deep way. They transgress, trespass without malice. Sometimes, they inhabit or even define the boundaries themselves--the ivy that grows on the wall, the hedge that *is* a wall.
