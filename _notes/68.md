I think there's an opportunity to do some sort of creative project that is organized around making connections between people who are at the same cafe at the same time using bluetooth or wifi or airdrop. 

The idea was prompted by being at a local cafe that doesn't have wifi, and seeing the list of "personal hotspots" when I went to connect to *my* hotspot: 

* "Frank's iPhone" 
* "iPhone (94)"
* "Sara's iPhone" 
* etc...

Similar lists come up when thinking of sharing something over airdrop or connecting to bluetooth. 

All these things are lists of co-presence, made visible through invisible technologies (electromagnetic waves!). 

I wonder how this could be a jumping off point to create new connections, while also not being "creepy". Something like: "Frank is here too! Do you want to say hi?". Or maybe: "do you want to invite 'Mary' to share your hotspot too?". I am not sure exactly what is the right form or entrypoint, but I think it's a curious space.
