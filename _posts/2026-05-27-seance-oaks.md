With my friend Elise, I have a piece in [Issue 7 of the Quinobequin Review](https://www.quinobequin.press/issue7). The issue theme is "Magic". Our piece is called "Seance for a Stand of Oaks" and is about the Waverley Oaks which once stood at [Beaver Brook Reservation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beaver_Brook_Reservation). The Q review is print-only, so I would encourage you to order a copy if you would like to read the piece (along with other great local art & writing).
