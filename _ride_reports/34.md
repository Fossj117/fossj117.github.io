E and I met at Dave's for sandwiches around noon and then rode out down the minuteman (after linear park detours). It's been warm recently, at least during the day, and it was a nice day for riding. We stopped at a bench by [Spy Pond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spy_Pond) to eat, and discussed reservoirs and college, and then continued on toward Arlington's Great Meadow. At the meadow we wandered, crossing paths with a large group of young children from a nearby school or daycare playing in the forest/meadow. The meadow is very brown in this season, but for few straggling green leaves here and there. We stopped by a favorite boulder of mine. On the way back we found the Arlington Community Orchard -- also a site that's maybe more exciting in the Spring and Summer. On the mushroom logs we spied one little mushroom near the bottom of a pile.
