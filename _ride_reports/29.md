First longer ride on the Bombora in Cambridge now that it's getting a bit warmer. I did [wet feet](https://www.urbandirt.org/routes/wet-feet). It's interesting to do something familiar after not doing it for a while. A lot was fairly green I guess after some recent rain. The "wet feet" part of the ride was fully flooded out, and so I had to detour around it: 

{% include image.html src="/figs/ride_reports/29/img1.jpg" alt="The wet feet section fully flooded out" %}

Overall, the route was chunkier than I had remembered. But still very nice; I'm looking forward to riding more this season.
