J and I did a casual ride around some of the rail trails near Olympia, WA. J was buzzing from a recent work happening. I felt tired but was happy to get outside. I shared my rule of always exploring at least one new pathway on a ride; this took us onto a short dirt path which led to a small lake with seating areas. We talked about how the trees were "new growth" but still very large. I like the trees and other flora of the Pacific Northwest. The ferns feel especially characteristic.
